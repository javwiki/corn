## Details

Emily Willis (born 1999) is an Argentine-American former adult film actress and model, born in San Rafael, Mendoza, Argentina. After her parents separated, she moved with her mother to St. George, Utah, at the age of seven and was raised in a Latter-day Saint household; she graduated from high school early and moved to San Diego, California.

She began shooting adult films in 2017. She moved to Las Vegas in early 2018 and later to Los Angeles, and in May 2019 was named Penthouse Pet of the Month.

In 2020 she won NightMoves Best Female Performer (Editor's Choice). In 2021 she won the AVN, XBIZ, and XRCO Female Performer of the Year awards; in 2022 she won XBIZ Female Performer of the Year again and shared the XRCO award with Gianna Dior.

In 2022 she joined the cast of Eddie Alcazar's *Divinity*, released in 2023, and appeared in the music video for Slipknot's single "Hive Mind"; she also launched a clothing line, EmFatale. In February 2024 she suffered a cardiac arrest that caused irreversible brain damage, ending her performing career.
