## Details

Lana Rhoades, born 6 September 1996 in Chicago, is an American internet personality, podcaster, and former adult film actress. She was raised by a single mother in a Chicago suburb alongside an older sister who has schizophrenia; the family was Southern Baptist and her grandfather was a preacher. At 16 she was sentenced to youth detention, where she earned her GED, and was released after a year for good behaviour.

She first worked as a hostess at a Tilted Kilt restaurant and performed at a strip club. In 2016, at 19, she appeared in her first adult film, and she left the adult film industry in late 2017. In 2019 she was the most-searched performer on Pornhub, with 345 million views. In February 2020 Brazzers hired her as a marketing consultant and creative collaborator.

After leaving the industry she became a social media influencer, posting on OnlyFans, co-hosting the podcast *3 Girls 1 Kitchen*, and designing a lingerie line with the Playboy-owned company Yandy; her YouTube channel has about 1 million subscribers and 21.3 million views. In 2021 she promoted the cryptocurrency PAWGcoin and in November of that year started an NFT image project, which launched in 2022; according to the cryptocurrency journalist Coffeezilla, the project was abandoned after its \$1.8 million launch and \$1.5 million worth of assets were transferred out. In January 2022 she announced the birth of her son.
