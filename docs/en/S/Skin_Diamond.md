## Career

Skin Diamond was born in Ventura, California, and raised in Dunfermline, Scotland, where her parents worked as missionaries. She is of Ethiopian, Danish, Czech, Yugoslavian, and German descent.

She began as an alternative model for GodsGirls before moving into art and fetish photography. In 2009 she won Bizarre magazine's first Cover Girl Search and appeared on its cover; that year she also entered the adult film industry, filming her first scenes with Joanna Angel and James Deen.

In 2011 she appeared in an i-D fashion editorial, and in 2012 comic artist David Mack portrayed her as Echo in the miniseries Daredevil: End of Days. She was a Penthouse Pet of the Month in July 2014 and made her directorial debut with Skin Diamond's Dollhouse.

After filming her final adult scenes in 2016, she moved into mainstream acting and music, playing Dylan Quinn in the Showtime series Submission and releasing music under her own name, Raylin Joy. Since 2024 she has been the lead singer of the band The Calamatix.
