## Details

Chanel Preston (born 1 December 1985) is an American adult film actress, director, and sex educator. She was born in Fairbanks, Alaska, and raised in the state.

She entered the industry in 2010 and won NightMoves Best New Starlet (Editor's Choice) in her debut year. In 2011 she took NightMoves Best Female Performer (Editor's Choice), the XBIZ New Starlet of the Year award, and the XRCO Best New Starlet award, the latter tied with Allie Haze. In March 2012 she became a Penthouse Pet of the Month, and from 2012 to 2016 she appeared on CNBC's "The Dirty Dozen" list of the most popular adult stars.

In 2013 she won the XBIZ award for Best Scene – Gonzo/Non-Feature Release for *Nacho Invades America 2* and was named NightMoves Best Female Performer (Fan's Choice). On 18 January 2014 she co-hosted the 31st AVN Awards with Samantha Saint, and that year won the AVN Most Outrageous Sex Scene award for *Get My Belt*. In March 2014 she launched the sex-education web series *Naked with Chanel*, funded through a crowdfunding campaign.

She has served as president of the Adult Performer Advocacy Committee and co-hosted the sex-positive podcast *SexThink* with Rob Nelson. She has also worked as a director since 2015, with 4 directing credits, and had 1431 performer credits listed on IAFD as of 2026.
