## Details

Carter Cruise (born 24 April 1991) is an American former adult film actress, DJ, record producer, and podcast host. She was born in Atlanta, Georgia, and raised in Cary, North Carolina. Home schooled as a child, she attended Cary High School and enrolled at East Carolina University in 2009, majoring in psychology and pre-law.

In the summer of 2013 she left university to enter the adult industry, making her debut that August after contacting the Florida-based agency East Coast Talents; she had previously worked as a Hooters girl and a lifeguard. She moved to Los Angeles in March 2014 and signed with Spiegler Girls that June, and was featured in AVN's "fresh" issue. That year she played the lead in Jacky St. James's romance feature *Second Chances*, produced by New Sensations.

In 2015 she became the second performer, after Jenna Jameson in 1996, to win AVN's Best New Starlet and Best Actress in the same year, and also took XBIZ Best New Starlet, XRCO New Starlet, and NightMoves Best New Starlet. In October 2015 she signed a one-year exclusive contract with Axel Braun Productions. In 2016 CNBC named her one of the dozen most popular adult stars.

From 2015 she gradually shifted toward music: she featured on Styles&Complete's single "DUNNIT", began DJing at college fraternity parties and released her debut single "Everybody Nose Me" in 2017, and issued her debut EP *Send Moods* in August 2018, followed by work on a second EP, *Sin Music*.
