## Career

Christy Mack grew up in Edinburgh, Indiana, where she was a cheerleader. She married at 18, later left her husband, and moved to Miami, where she began modeling and adult film work.

She entered the industry in 2011. In 2012 she played the DC Comics character Zatanna in the adult parody The Dark Knight XXX. She launched her official website with Puba in April 2013, and Fleshlight released her signature products that November.

In 2013 she was named Miss FreeOnes, won the FreeOnes Best Newcomer award, and received the Venus Award for Best Actress International. The following year she won XBIZ Best New Starlet and the AVN Most Promising New Starlet fan award.

In August 2014 Mack was seriously injured in an assault at her home by her former boyfriend, who was later convicted. She confirmed her retirement from adult film performance in 2015 and in 2016 launched a clothing line with Pornhub to raise money for domestic violence survivors.
