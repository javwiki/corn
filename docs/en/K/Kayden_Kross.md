# Kayden Kross

## Summary

- **Name**: Kayden Kross
- **Platforms**: X / Instagram / Official website
- **Born**: September 15, 1985, Sacramento, California
- **Years active**: 2006–present
- **Occupation**: Adult film performer, director, and writer
- **Notes**: Co-founded Deeper in 2019; member of the AVN and XRCO halls of fame

## Career

Kayden Kross grew up in Sacramento and was raised by her mother in a strict religious household. She studied psychology and philosophy at California State University, Sacramento, and at 18 worked at Rick's Showgirls in Rancho Cordova.

She entered the adult film industry in 2006 and was signed successively by Vivid, Adam & Eve, and Digital Playground. In September 2008 she became a Penthouse Pet of the Month and launched ClubKayden.com; in 2010 she starred in Digital Playground's Body Heat and won two best actress awards for the role.

Kross co-hosted the 2010 AVN Awards with Kirsten Price and Dave Attell and hosted the XBIZ Awards again in 2012. In 2019 she helped launch Deeper, a platform within Vixen Media Group.

Her directing work has won AVN, XBIZ, XRCO, and NightMoves awards, including AVN Best Director – Non-Feature in 2018 and XBIZ Director of the Year – Feature in 2019. She was inducted into the AVN Hall of Fame in 2019 and the XRCO Hall of Fame in 2022.
