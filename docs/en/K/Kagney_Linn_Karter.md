## Details

Kagney Linn Karter (March 28, 1987 – February 15, 2024) was an American adult film actress, exotic dancer, and model. Born in Harris County, Texas, she grew up in St. Joseph, Missouri, and Ridgway, Pennsylvania. In 2007, she was named Missouri's Déjà Vu Showgirl of the Year, then moved to California and modeled for photographer Holly Randall.

She entered the adult film industry in September 2008; her first scene was with Johnny Sins for Naughty America. In June 2009, she was named Penthouse Pet of the Month and appeared on the covers of Hustler and AVN. In January 2010, she signed an exclusive performing contract with Zero Tolerance Entertainment. That year she won Best New Starlet at the AVN Awards, New Starlet of the Year at the XBIZ Awards, and New Starlet at the XRCO Awards. She also appeared in parody films including Not Married With Children XXX and Official Silence of the Lambs Parody, and in Louis Theroux's 2012 documentary Twilight of the Porn Stars.

She continued to perform into the 2020s, with 1044 credits listed by IAFD. On February 15, 2024, she died at her home near Cleveland, Ohio, at the age of 36.
