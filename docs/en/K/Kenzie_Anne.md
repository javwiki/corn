## Details

Kenzie Anne was born on 9 March 1993 in Newbury Park, California. She began performing in adult films in 2020 and within two years had become a regular lead in studio features and vignettes. IAFD records 212 credits between 2020 and 2026.

Her breakthrough came in 2022, when she was named Best New Starlet at the NightMoves Awards and Best New Performer at the XBIZ Awards; the XBIZ category is also summarised in some award lists as Best New Starlet. At the same XBIZ ceremony she won Best Sex Scene – Vignette for *Black and White 19*.
