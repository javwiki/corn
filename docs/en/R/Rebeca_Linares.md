## Career

Rebeca Linares was born in San Sebastián in Spain's Basque Country and moved to Barcelona with her mother at age four. She began appearing in adult films in Spain in 2005; because work was limited there, she also filmed in Berlin and France.

She moved to Los Angeles in March 2006. In 2008 she shot a photo spread for Maxim, and in March 2009 she was a Penthouse Pet of the Month. That year the Spanish television channel Canal+ made a documentary about her career, Vente a Las Vegas, Nena.

In 2007 she won the FICEB Ninfa Award for Best Supporting Actress for Iodine Girl. In January 2010 she shared the AVN Award for Best Three-Way Sex Scene with Tori Black and Mark Ashley for Tori Black Is Pretty Filthy, and she was named Urban X Female Performer of the Year.

Linares appeared in nearly 700 scenes between 2005 and 2011 before taking a hiatus. She returned to the industry in December 2023.
