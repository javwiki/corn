## Details

Riley Reid (born 9 July 1991) is an American adult film actress born in Florida. She briefly worked as a stripper before beginning to shoot films around 2010, initially under the name Paige Riley.

In 2012 she won NightMoves Best New Starlet (Editor's Choice). She won XBIZ Best New Starlet in 2013 and XBIZ Female Performer of the Year in 2014, becoming the first performer to take those two XBIZ honors in consecutive years. In 2016 she won AVN Female Performer of the Year.

In 2018 she won the AVN Social Media Star award and the Pornhub Most Popular Female Performer award, and took XBIZ Performer Site of the Year for her personal website; she won the Pornhub Most Popular Female Performer award again in 2022. She was inducted into the XRCO Hall of Fame in 2021 and the AVN Hall of Fame in 2025.

In 2023 she launched Clona, an AI virtual companion platform offering interactive AI versions of participating creators.
