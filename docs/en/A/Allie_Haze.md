## Details

Allie Haze was born on 10 May 1987. She entered the adult film industry in 2009 and worked steadily for fifteen years, appearing in productions for a wide range of studios and websites. IAFD records 648 credits between 2009 and 2024.

Her parody work brought her a broad audience. She played the lead in *Star Wars XXX: A Porn Parody* (2012), a role that earned her an AVN nomination for Best Actress, and she also appeared in *Clerks XXX: A Porn Parody*. In 2015 she won the AVN Award for Best Three-Way Sex Scene (G/B/B) for *Allie*.

Haze was named Best New Starlet at both the 2011 AVN Awards and the 2011 XRCO Awards, and also took the Editor's Choice prize in the same category at the 2011 NightMoves Awards. She retired from performing in 2024.
