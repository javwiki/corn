- **Active years**: 2007 – 2019 (IAFD)

- **Notes**: Caucasian/Native American; 5'6" (167 cm), 123 lb (56 kg), measurements 34B-25-34

## Details

Andy San Dimas was born on 3 October 1986 in Baltimore, Maryland. She began performing in adult films in 2007 and became known for an alternative, heavily tattooed look that set her apart from the mainstream starlet image of the period. IAFD records 492 credits between 2007 and 2019.

Her best-known work came in parody and feature productions. She won the 2011 AVN Award for Best Actress for *This Ain't Glee XXX*, sharing the category with India Summer, and the 2011 XBIZ Award for Female Performer of the Year. The following year she took the AVN Award for Best POV Sex Scene for *Double Vision 3*.

San Dimas retired from performing in 2019.
