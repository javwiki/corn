# Major industry awards

This page is a reference directory of the main awards and awarding bodies in the adult film industry. It supports the "Main awards" sections in performer entries. Award names, ceremony years, and categories should be checked against each organiser's published lists; wins and nominations in the same category must always be recorded separately.

## Selection and record-keeping

- **Wins versus nominations:** a nomination is not a win. The "Result" column in an entry's table should say either "Won" or "Nominated".
- **Year convention:** use the year of the ceremony by default. If a source attributes an award to the year a work was released, state that convention in the entry.
- **Category names:** keep the organiser's English category name so it can be checked against the official list; a Chinese translation may be added for readers.
- **Fan awards versus industry awards:** Pornhub, the NightMoves fan categories, and Fleshbot are primarily fan-voted; AVN, XBIZ, and XRCO are primarily judged by industry or critics' panels. Do not treat them as equivalent.
- **Hiatuses and revivals:** GayVN and Urban X were paused and later revived, so cross-year comparisons should account for the gap in ceremonies.
- **Vertical awards:** smaller or niche awards such as the Spank Bank, AltStar, and Inked Awards also appear in performer entries; check their organiser lists in the same way.
