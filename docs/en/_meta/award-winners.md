# Award winners by year

This page lists winners of selected flagship categories, award by award and year by year, as a reference for the "Awards" sections in performer entries. The lists cover only part of the awards and main competitive categories and are **not intended to be complete**; nominations are excluded. See [Major industry awards](awards.md) for the awarding bodies and official lists.

## How to read this page

- **Year:** the year of the ceremony by default; Pornhub Awards are recorded by announcement year.
- **Ties:** multiple winners in the same category are separated by a slash.
- **Gaps:** `—` means the category was not presented that year, no award was given, or this page has not collected it.
- **Names:** category names keep the organiser's English wording; the Chinese edition adds Chinese glosses.
- **Verification:** this page is a finding aid, not a substitute for official lists. Check the organiser's announcement before citing a win in an entry.
- **As of:** October 2026.
