## Details

Gianna Dior, born 12 May 1997 in Auburn, Alabama, is an American adult film actress and model of Italian and Native American descent. She studied psychology at Auburn University and worked part-time in a sushi restaurant and at an eye clinic. In May 2018 a Miami agent noticed her on Tinder and invited her to shoot adult scenes, and she made her debut at 21; her stage name was chosen by that agent to recall her Italian origins.

In September 2018 she was named Penthouse Pet of the Month and in 2019 Penthouse Pet of the Year. In 2020 she won Best New Starlet at both the AVN and XRCO Awards and, with Mick Blue, the AVN award for Best Boy/Girl Sex Scene for *Unlocked*; the same year she shot her first anal scene for Evil Angel. At the 2022 AVN Awards she won four trophies, including Female Performer of the Year, becoming the third actress ever to take that title after winning Best New Starlet; the XRCO Female Performer of the Year award that year was a tie between her and Emily Willis.

In May 2024 she directed a scene for Vixen Media Group, beginning her directing work after appearing in more than 20 of the company's films. In March 2021 she was temporarily paralysed when her body rejected vitamin B12 and her spinal cord was damaged, and she recovered after about a month. In October 2021 Emily Willis filed a \$5 million defamation suit against her; the case was dismissed in 2025.
