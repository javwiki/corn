## Details

Mia Khalifa, born Sarah Joe Chamoun on 10 February 1993 in Beirut, Lebanon, grew up in a conservative Catholic family and attended a private French-language school in Beirut. In 2001, because of the conflict in southern Lebanon, she moved with her family to Montgomery County, Maryland, United States, and played field hockey during high school. She later attended the University of Texas at El Paso (UTEP), earned a bachelor's degree in history, and supported herself through bartending, modeling, and working as a "suitcase girl" on the Spanish-language version of *Deal or No Deal*.

In October 2014, Khalifa entered the adult film industry. A Bang Bros scene in which she wore an Islamic hijab attracted worldwide attention and controversy. Although her adult-film career lasted only about three months, she quickly became Pornhub's most-searched performer and was named Pornhub's most popular performer in 2015 and 2018. xHamster also named her its most-searched adult actress in 2016. The industry calls this the "Streisand effect": attempts to suppress her instead accelerated the spread of her reputation.

After leaving the adult industry, Khalifa became a webcam model, OnlyFans creator, and sports commentator. She worked with Complex Networks to host the daily sports talk show *Sportsball*, which aired on Rooster Teeth. She also became an activist, publicly advocating for sex workers, and has spoken candidly on Twitter about political issues such as the Israeli-Palestinian conflict, prompting broad public debate. In 2023, she launched her own jewelry line.

On social media, Mia Khalifa has about 6.3 million Twitter/X followers (@miakhalifa), with content focused on political commentary, cultural issues, and personal life; she is also active on Instagram. She founded Sheytan.World. Khalifa has publicly described being bullied at school because of her ethnicity and appearance, especially after the September 11 attacks, as well as the breakdown of her relationship with her family over her career choices.
