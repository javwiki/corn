## Details

Tori Black (born 1988) is an American adult film actress born in Seattle, Washington. She studied journalism at Western Washington University.

She entered the industry at 18 after contacting an adult talent agency during a summer holiday in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and began shooting in 2007. In December 2008 she was named Penthouse Pet of the Month.

In 2010 she won the AVN, XBIZ, and XRCO Female Performer of the Year awards; in 2011 she again won the AVN and XRCO awards, becoming the first performer to win AVN Female Performer of the Year two years running. She also played Catwoman in Vivid Entertainment's *Batman XXX: A Porn Parody*.

In 2014 she guest-starred in the second season of the Showtime series *Ray Donovan* and made her directorial debut; she later appeared in *L.A. Slasher* (2015) and *American Satan* (2017). In 2018 she won XBIZ and NightMoves awards for *Tori Black Is Back*, as well as the XRCO Best Cumback award.
