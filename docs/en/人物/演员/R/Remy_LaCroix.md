## Details

Remy LaCroix (born 26 June 1988) is an American adult film actress born in San Francisco, California. Before entering the industry she worked as a specialty dancer at music festivals and Burning Man, performing fire dancing, aerial silks, and hula hoop.

She made her debut in December 2011 in a Kink.com production. She announced her departure about six months later but honored existing commitments, worked in Kink.com's talent department, and returned to shooting in November 2012. In 2013 she won XBIZ Best Actress – Couples-Themed Release for *Torn*, along with AVN Best New Starlet and XRCO New Starlet.

In 2014 she won AVN Best Actress for *The Temptation of Eve*, XRCO Female Performer of the Year, and NightMoves Best Female Performer (Editor's Choice). In December 2014 she signed with ArchAngel Productions and terminated the contract less than three months later.

She stopped performing in 2016 due to pregnancy and served as a judge on the web reality series *The Sex Factor*; she announced a return in 2020 and shot a comeback scene for Bellesa in 2022.
