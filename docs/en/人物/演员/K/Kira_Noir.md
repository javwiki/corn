## Details

Kira Noir was born on 16 July 1994 in San Marino, California, and was raised in Nashville, Tennessee. After graduating from high school she worked as a stripper, including at Larry Flynt's Hustler Club in St. Louis, before entering the adult film industry in 2014. She has cited Bonnie Rotten, Skin Diamond and Stoya as inspirations.

Noir's awards run from scene and supporting work to the industry's top performer prize. She won the AVN Award for Best Supporting Actress in 2021, 2022 and 2023, and was named AVN Female Performer of the Year in 2023. In 2024 she took Best Leading Actress at the AVN Awards and Best Actress at the XRCO and NightMoves awards for *Machine Gunner*. She signed an exclusive contract with Brazzers in 2023 and co-hosted the 38th AVN Awards digitally with Skyler Lo.

Outside performing, Noir has spoken about consent and about the treatment of Black women in the industry, and in 2025 she was elected to the board of the Performer Availability Screening Service (PASS). Murals of her have been painted in Kenya, the country of her family's origin. IAFD records 951 credits between 2014 and 2026.
