## Details

Kenzie Taylor was born on 2 July 1990 in Midland, Michigan. She entered the adult film industry in 2015 and built one of the largest filmographies of her cohort, with IAFD recording 922 credits through 2026. Since 2022 she has also worked as a director.

Taylor's award recognition has come from the NightMoves Awards. She was named Best New Starlet (Fan's Choice) in 2016, Best Actress (Editor's Choice) in 2019, and Best Actress again in 2022.
