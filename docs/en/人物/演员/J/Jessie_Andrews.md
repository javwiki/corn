## Details

Jessie Andrews (born March 22, 1992, per IAFD; Wikipedia gives only 1991 or 1992) is an American actress, model, jewelry designer, and former adult film actress. Born in Miami, Florida, she is of Chinese, Irish, and Latvian descent. In high school she worked as a sales associate at American Apparel; a week after her 18th birthday, she began performing in adult films.

In 2011, she starred in Elegant Angel's Portrait of a Call Girl, winning Best Actress at the 2012 AVN Awards; that year she also received XBIZ's New Starlet of the Year and XRCO's New Starlet. Her adult film work ran from 2010 to 2017, with 409 credits listed by IAFD.

In 2012, she founded the jewelry line Bagatiba and later launched Basic Swim and Jeu Illimité; she also toured as a DJ in North America, Asia, and Australia. After 2017, she moved into mainstream film and television, appearing in Hot Summer Nights (2017) and Euphoria (2022). In 2025, she starred in Love Bomb and wrote, directed, and acted in the short film Tell Me Something.
