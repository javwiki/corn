## Career

Jesse Jane was born in Fort Worth, Texas, and raised in Moore, Oklahoma, where she graduated with honors from Moore High School in 1998. She worked for Hooters and Hawaiian Tropic and became a regional training coordinator.

In 2002 she signed an exclusive contract with Digital Playground and became one of the studio's best-known contract performers, starring in Pirates (2005) and Pirates II: Stagnetti's Revenge (2008). She also appeared on mainstream television programs including Entourage and The Tonight Show with Jay Leno.

Her awards included 9 AVN Awards, 5 NightMoves Awards, and 4 XBIZ Awards, for a total of 37 wins. She was inducted into the NightMoves Hall of Fame in 2008, the XRCO Hall of Fame in 2012, and the AVN Hall of Fame in 2013.

Jane retired from the adult industry in 2017 and returned in 2019. On January 24, 2024, she and her boyfriend were found dead at their home in Moore, Oklahoma; an autopsy report attributed her death to an accidental overdose of cocaine and fentanyl.
