## Career

Alexis Texas was born at a U.S. naval base in the Panama Canal Zone and raised in Castroville, Texas. She is of Puerto Rican, Norwegian, and German descent, studied respiratory care at Texas State University, and first encountered the adult industry while working at a restaurant at 21.

She filmed her first scene in October 2006, went on to work for Bang Bros and other companies, and moved to Los Angeles in March 2007. Belladonna directed Discovering Alexis Texas (2008), the same year Texas won the NightMoves Award for Best New Starlet (Fan's Choice). In 2009 she launched her own company and official website; Alexis Texas Is Buttwoman earned her AVN and XRCO awards for all-sex and gonzo releases.

In 2010 she received the F.A.M.E. Award for Favorite Ass and was named by Maxim among the industry's leading performers. She won the NightMoves Best Female Performer (Fan's Choice) award in 2011. CNBC included her among the most popular performers in 2012, when she signed an exclusive contract with Adam & Eve; by then she had appeared in more than 400 adult films.

Texas appeared in the 2013 documentary Aroused and, via archival footage, in the film Don Jon. She signed an exclusive directing contract with Elegant Angel in 2015; her directorial debut, Big Booty Tryouts, followed that year, and she co-hosted the 2015 AVN Awards with Kirsten Price and Dave Attell. She was inducted into the AVN Hall of Fame in 2022.
