## Details

Abella Danger was born on 19 November 1995 in Miami, Florida. She made her adult-film debut in July 2014 for Bang Bros and later moved to Los Angeles. *Fortune* has described her as one of the most popular and in-demand performers in the business.

Her credits span the major studios and websites, and her work has extended into mainstream pop culture. In 2019 she appeared in *Her & Him*, Bella Thorne's directorial debut for Pornhub, after also featuring in the series' earlier film *I Love You*. She contributed vocals to and appeared in the music video for Borgore's "911", and in 2021 she starred in Thorne's "Shake It" video. In 2022 she recreated the pool scene from *Wild Things* with Denise Richards, and in January 2023 she co-hosted the AVN Awards with Reya Sunshine.

The 2016 awards season brought broad recognition: she won Best New Starlet at the AVN, XBIZ, and XRCO Awards, as well as Best Female Performer (Editor's Choice) at the NightMoves Awards. She went on to win AVN fan awards for Most Epic Ass and Most Amazing Ass, and was named Most Popular Female Performer at the fifth Pornhub Awards in 2023; she was reported to have received more than 1.89 billion views on Pornhub that year. Since 2024 she has studied at the University of Miami, with the goal of becoming an attorney or a sports agent.
