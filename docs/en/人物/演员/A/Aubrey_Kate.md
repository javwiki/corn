## Details

Aubrey Kate (born 7 November 1990) is an American transgender adult film actress and director from Orange County, California. She began gender transition at 17 and entered the adult film industry in 2013, becoming one of the most recognizable performers in contemporary transgender adult cinema.

Her work has been released mainly through studios such as Grooby, Evil Angel, and Trans Angels, spanning live-action features and VR scenes. Notable titles include *Aubrey Kate: TS Superstar*, *I Am Aubrey*, *Captain Marvel XXX: An Axel Braun Parody*, and *A Cab Named Desire*. Known for her blonde, tall appearance and steady output, she had 480 performer credits listed on IAFD as of 2026.

In awards terms, she first won AVN's Transgender Performer of the Year in 2017 and retained it in 2018, then won again in 2021 and 2026, making her the most frequent winner in the category's history. She has also received the 2017 XBIZ Transgender Performer of the Year award, the 2019 XRCO Trans Performer of the Year award, NightMoves Best Transsexual Performer in 2018, 2021, and 2022, and the 2019 Pornhub Award for Favorite Trans Model.

From 2025 she also began working as a director, with 21 directing credits. At the 2026 AVN Awards she took the performer prize for a fourth time and also won Best Trans One-on-One Sex Scene for *A Cab Named Desire*, Best Trans Group Sex Scene for *Let Them Eat Cock*, and Best VR Trans Sex Scene for *The Boss!
