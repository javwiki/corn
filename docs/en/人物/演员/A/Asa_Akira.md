## Details

Asa Akira was born in Manhattan and grew up in SoHo; she is of Japanese descent. She spent four years of her childhood in Tokyo and attended private schools in New York and Japan. At 19 she began working as a dominatrix, later dancing at the Hustler Club in New York. From 2006 to 2007 she was a regular on the *Bubba the Love Sponge* radio show. Her first boy-girl scene was shot by Gina Lynn Productions with Travis Knight, after several girl-girl scenes, mainly with Lynn; she then signed with Vouyer Media before going freelance six months later.

In 2009 she received several award nominations for her role in David Aaron Clark's *Pure*. In January 2013 she co-hosted the 30th AVN Awards with Jesse Jane and comedian April Macie, and won AVN Female Performer of the Year that night, becoming the third Asian performer to do so after Asia Carrera and Stephanie Swift and the most awarded person at the ceremony. That year she made her directorial debut with Elegant Angel's *Gangbanged 6*, and in October 2013 she signed an exclusive performing contract with Wicked Pictures, debuting in *Asa Is Wicked*.

In mainstream media, Akira published the memoir *Insatiable: Porn—A Love Story* with Grove Press in May 2014, followed by the essay collection *Dirty Thirty* in 2016 and the anthology *Asarotica*, which she edited, in 2017. She hosted the first and second Pornhub Awards and the 2016 online reality show *The Sex Factor*, and appeared as herself in the sixteenth-season premiere of *Family Guy* in 2017. She has been inducted into the AVN, XRCO, Urban X and Brazzers halls of fame, and was married to performer and director Toni Ribas from 2012 to 2017.
