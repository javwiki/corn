## Details

Adriana Chechik was born in Downingtown, Pennsylvania. Before entering the adult industry she worked as a dancer at Scarlett's Cabaret in Hallandale Beach, Florida. In May 2013 she signed a one-year, non-exclusive contract with Erotique Entertainment, marking her debut. On 5 September 2014 she launched her own website on the Cherry Pimps Network; that August she appeared on *The Howard Stern Show*, and the following month she was featured in *Cosmopolitan*.

The years from 2015 to 2021 brought most of her awards. She won the 2015 AVN Award for Best Anal Sex Scene for *Internal Damnation 8*, then took XRCO Female Performer of the Year in both 2016 and 2017, and AVN Female Performer of the Year in 2017. She also collected AVN awards for oral, group and "most outrageous" scenes, and from 2016 onward was repeatedly named Best Female Performer at the NightMoves Awards. In 2019 she appeared in an episode of the horror-parody series *LadyKiller TV*.

Around 2022 Chechik largely retired from performing and moved to video-game streaming on Twitch. On 10 October 2022 she jumped into a shallow foam-cube pit at a TwitchCon exhibit; because the pit was not properly padded, she fractured her spine in two places and underwent surgery to have a rod implanted, documenting a long recovery on stream. In 2023 she partnered with Forever Voices AI on an AI companion service built on her voice, and in November 2025 she said she had resumed filming after physical therapy.
