## Details

Vanna Bardot, born 3 February 1999 in Denver, Colorado, is an American adult film actress who grew up in Miami, Florida. Before entering the industry she wanted to be a hairstylist and worked in salons from 15 until 18, then worked as a ballet and club dancer in Miami; her stage name came from a crush she had in middle school.

She entered the industry in 2018 at about 19, was named Cherry Pimps Cherry of the Month in November 2020 and Bang! Babe of the Month in January 2021, and received AVN Award nominations in both 2020 and 2021. In 2022 she signed an exclusive contract with Slayed, a division of Vixen Media Group, and became one of the company's frequent faces; she was also crowned a Vixen Angel in 2023. In 2023 she played herself in *Influence: Vanna Bardot*, directed by Kayden Kross, which became a signature title of her career.

In 2023 she won the XBIZ Female Performer of the Year award; in 2024 she won Female Performer of the Year from AVN, XBIZ, and XRCO, taking AVN honours for Best Anal Sex Scene, Best Double-Penetration Sex Scene, and Best Music for *Influence: Vanna Bardot*, as well as Best Girl/Girl Sex Scene for *Punch*. She also appeared in the 2023 comedy film *The Wine Club* and in the Adult Time series *Cardiogasm*.
