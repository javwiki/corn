## Details

Venus Lux was born on 10 October 1990 in San Francisco, California. She began performing in adult films in 2012 and became one of the most prominent transgender performers and producers of the following decade. IAFD records 422 credits between 2012 and 2023.

Lux won the AVN Award for Transsexual Performer of the Year in 2015 and 2016, and also took Best Transsexual Sex Scene in 2015 for *TS, I Love You*. The XBIZ Awards named her Transsexual Performer of the Year in 2015, and she won Best Transexual Performer at the 2014 NightMoves Awards. Her earlier scene work was recognised at the 2013 Tranny Awards, where she won Best Hardcore Performer, Best Scene for *Asian Nail Salon* and Best Solo Website; she won the website category again in 2016 and received a Lifetime Achievement award in 2021.

She owns the studio Venus Lux Entertainment, which signed a three-year exclusive distribution deal with Pulse Distribution in December 2014. She wrote the column "Venus Rising", collected in the book *Venus Lux Diaries* (2015), launched *TransGlobal Magazine* in October 2015, and founded the Syren Network and TS Fetishes in 2016. She also appears in the documentary *After Porn Ends 2*.
