## Details

Maitland Ward was born Ashley Maitland Welkos on 3 February 1977 in Long Beach, California. She first became known as a mainstream actress, playing Jessica Forrester on *The Bold and the Beautiful* from 1994 to 1996 and Rachel McGuire on *Boy Meets World* from 1998 to 2000; she also appeared in the 2004 film *White Chicks*. After retiring from mainstream acting she moved into cosplay and convention appearances.

Ward began performing in adult films in 2019, making her debut in *Drive* for Deeper.com after signing with the Society 15 agency. The role won her the 2020 AVN Award for Best Supporting Actress, and she followed it with Best Actress at the 2021 AVN Awards for *Muse Season 1*. In 2022 she was named XBIZ Performer of the Year, and in 2023 she won Best Leading Actress at the AVN Awards for *Drift*. She added Best Actress – Featurette wins in 2024 and 2025.

Her memoir, *Rated X: How Porn Liberated Me from Hollywood*, was published by Atria Books in 2022. IAFD records 98 adult credits between 2019 and 2025, plus directing work from 2022 to 2023.
