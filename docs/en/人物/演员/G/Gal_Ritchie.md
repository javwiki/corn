Gal Ritchie (born 3 May 2001 in England) is a British adult film actress and model. She grew up in a village of about 2,000 people near Brighton in southern England, began performing on a live cam site at 18, trained at the London School of Domination, and worked as a professional dominatrix and software-engineering recruiter.

In August 2023 she moved to Los Angeles to enter adult film, shooting her first scene for Team Skeet with Charles Dera. Her breakout year was 2024, when she appeared for Brazzers, Reality Kings, Vixen Media Group, Girlsway, Jules Jordan, Evil Angel, and Kink.com, and was cast in Digital Playground's *Gold Diggers*.
