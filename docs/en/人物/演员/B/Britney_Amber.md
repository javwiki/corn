## Details

Britney Amber was born on 10 November 1986 in Banning, California. She began performing in adult films in 2008 and became one of the industry's most prolific performers, with IAFD recording 1,440 credits through 2025 across studio productions and web scenes.

Her award recognition came mainly from the NightMoves Awards, where she was named Best Female Performer in the Editor's Choice category in both 2013 and 2018. Those remain her two recorded wins in the award lists collected on this site.
