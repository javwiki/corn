## Details

Bailey Jay was born on 5 November 1988 in Richmond, Virginia. She began working in adult films in 2008 and became one of the best-known transgender performers of her generation, combining film work with modeling, presenting and podcasting. IAFD records 430 credits between 2008 and 2025.

Jay won the AVN Award for Transsexual Performer of the Year in both 2011 and 2012, and in 2016 received the AVN Fan Award for Favorite Trans Performer. Her earlier work also earned Best Solo Model at the 2010 Transgender Erotica Awards and Best Internet Personality at the 2015 edition of the same awards.

Outside adult films she co-hosts several podcasts, including *The Bailey Jay Show* with her husband, photographer Matthew Terhune, *The Trans Witching Hour*, *Blood Lust* and *Sugar and Spice*. In 2014 she worked as a presenter for Vice Media on *The Jim Norton Show*, appearing in all four episodes before its cancellation. She has been married to Terhune since 2011.
