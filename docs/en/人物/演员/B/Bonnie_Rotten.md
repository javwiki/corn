## Details

Bonnie Rotten was born in May 1993 in Cincinnati, Ohio. She began as a fetish model for *Girls and Corpses* after winning the Ms. Dead Indiana contest at the Days of the Dead convention, and also worked as a model at car and motorcycle shows. She entered the adult industry in early 2012 and took her stage name from a pin-up zombie tattooed on her right leg.

Her early films for Digital Sin, *Meet Bonnie* and *The Gangbang of Bonnie Rotten*, established her as a leading figure in alt-porn. In 2014 she became the first alt-porn performer to win the AVN Award for Female Performer of the Year, and she also won Best Group Sex Scene that year for *The Gangbang of Bonnie Rotten*. She collected further honours from the XRCO, Inked, Venus and NightMoves awards, including Best Female Performer at NightMoves in 2014.

Rotten launched her own production company, Mental Beauty, Inc., in 2014 and made her directorial debut with *To the Core*. She stepped back from performing in 2015, returned in 2018, and retired in 2020. IAFD continues to list credits through 2023.
