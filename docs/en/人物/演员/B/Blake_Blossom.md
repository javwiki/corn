## Details

Blake Blossom is an American adult film actress from California; no reliable birth date is recorded in public sources. She began making amateur adult content in 2019, launched an OnlyFans account in February 2020, and made her professional debut in March 2020 in a scene for *Exploited College Girls*.

In December 2020 she was featured as Penthouse's Pet of the Month. In April 2021, XBIZ reported that she starred in *Skirt Sale* for the Deeper label, directed by Kayden Kross. She has since worked with Deeper, Vixen, Jules Jordan, Brazzers, Blacked, and Evil Angel, building a reputation on her natural blonde look, curvy figure, and prolific output, with 863 credits listed on IAFD as of 2026, including a large body of VR work.

She won Best New Starlet (Editor's Choice) at the 2021 NightMoves Awards. Her breakthrough year was 2022, when she took AVN's Best New Starlet and Hottest Newcomer, the XRCO New Starlet award, and NightMoves Best Female Performer. In 2023 she won the AVN Award for Best Actress – Featurette for *An Honest Man*. She has also been nominated several times for AVN Female Performer of the Year.
