## Details

Scarlit Scandal was born on 27 April 1999 in Fort Pierce, Florida, and is a native of West Palm Beach. At the 38th AVN Awards, the first all-digital edition of the ceremony, she won Best New Starlet. In 2023 Scandal signed an exclusive contract with Brazzers. The previous year she had appeared in an advertisement for the sneaker brand Rose In Good Faith that used recycled sex toys in the design.
